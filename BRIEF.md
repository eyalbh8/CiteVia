# Citevia — current brand and website brief

The active site is the Next.js application in `src/`. The historical Zyqon draft is retained in `docs/legacy-brand-brief.md` for reference only.

- Brand: **Citevia**, using the supplied Citevia.io logos.
- Contact: **eyal@citevia.com**.
- Default appearance: white, independent of system theme; an explicit preference may switch to dark.
- Palette: white `#FFFFFF`, indigo `#3930E9`, cyan `#00CDE6`, and blue accents. Text uses deep blue `#151940` and muted blue-gray `#545D7C`.
- Typography: locally served Instrument Serif headings, with Inter and Manrope variable fonts.
- Audience: complex B2B purchases and specialist sectors: yachting, private aviation, luxury real estate, jewellery and watches, and hospitality.
- Positioning: make useful expertise easier to find, understand, and evaluate in AI search.
- Voice: clear, specific, evidence-led; no guaranteed rankings or fabricated results.
- Assets: three original PNGs in `public/logo/`; historical Zyqon assets remain in `logo/`.
- Content: service, solution, article, example-study, and resource models in `src/content/`; page components supply the surrounding narrative.
- Editorial expansion: `/blog` has ten articles (seven original guides and three research articles adapted from the supplied Arielos source), `/solutions` has three audience pages, and `/case-studies` has six explicitly illustrative examples, including three detailed sector narratives with synthetic sample metrics. `/resources` and `/ai-info` provide learning and company references.
- Discovery: sitemap and LLM text files are generated from published content. White remains the default theme throughout.

See `docs/CONTENT-AND-SEARCH.md` for editorial standards and `docs/OPERATIONS.md` for deployment.
