### Process
- Eyebrow: How it works
- Headline: A five-step framework from audit to attributed leads.
- Subheadline: The steps run in sequence, then repeat. Each one leaves you with a deliverable you can inspect.
1. **Audit** — We analyze how AI engines currently describe your brand, your competitors, and your category across the prompts buyers actually use. Output: Visibility baseline
2. **Strategy** — We identify prompt opportunities, content gaps, entity issues, and authority needs, then rank them by revenue potential and effort. Output: Quarterly GEO plan
3. **Execution** — We build and optimize the pages, content, structured data, and supporting signals that engines can read, verify, and cite. Output: Shipped pages and fixes
4. **Expansion** — We strengthen third-party presence and brand credibility through coverage, listings, and references in trusted sources. Output: Authority footprint
5. **Measurement** — We track mentions, citations, share of answer, traffic, and leads, and report what moved, why, and what comes next. Output: Monthly growth report

## 6. Logo
A Q ring opened at the lower right so the Z's bottom stroke runs straight out as the Q's tail: one continuous stroke that reads as "the answer, delivered". Gradient mint→periwinkle on dark, teal→indigo on light, or `currentColor` mono. Files and usage rules in `logo/README.md`.

## 7. Font pairing
- Display and body: **Inter** (400/500/600/700, optical sizing on) — headlines at 700 with tight tracking (−0.03em to −0.04em) and the `cv11` / `ss01` / `ss03` alternates for a sharper, more geometric look; copy, nav and buttons at 400–600
- Data: **JetBrains Mono** (400/500) — numbers, eyebrows, axis labels, captions
- Both are on Google Fonts; fallbacks are system-ui and a monospace stack.
